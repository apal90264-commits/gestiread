Project Overview

What is GestiRead?

GestiRead is an inclusive learning platform built for educational accessibility. It aims to reduce the gap between sign language communication and traditional text-heavy learning systems by offering a smarter, faster, and more scalable digital bridge.

🤟

🧠

Problem Analysis

The Accessibility Gap in Education

Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners face barriers that go beyond hearing loss. The challenge includes classroom communication, self-study, comprehension of written language, interpreter scarcity, and limited access to always-available support systems.

Key Challenges

Communication isolation: Many students struggle to participate fully in standard classrooms where spoken instruction dominates.
Interpreter scarcity: Live sign interpreters are expensive, limited in number, and often unavailable during online learning or self-study.
Text comprehension difficulty: Sign languages have grammatical structures that differ from written language, which creates literacy and syntax barriers.
Static educational content: Traditional books, PDFs, and notes rarely provide sign-assisted explanations or dynamic accessibility support.

Why This Matters Nationally

Inclusive education is not only a social responsibility but also a national development priority. A platform like GestiRead supports equal learning opportunity, digital accessibility, and scalable educational support infrastructure.

Core Solution

Three Pillars of the GestiRead Platform

The system is designed as a multimodal stack. Instead of solving just one part of accessibility, it connects communication, reading, and learning reinforcement into one integrated experience.

🎙️

Real-Time Expression to Voice

Hand landmarks, facial cues, and pose signals are continuously tracked and processed to identify dynamic sign sequences. The recognized output is transformed into voice to help hearing educators and peers understand signed communication.

🔎

Readable Documents for Sign Learners

OCR extracts printed or digital text, while NLP modules simplify or reinterpret difficult passages into more accessible formats. This reduces dependency on manual explanation for every page of content.

🌍

Scalable Inclusive Learning

The platform can be used on laptops, webcams, and mobile devices, enabling broader deployment in classrooms, homes, and digital learning systems with lower operational dependency on human intermediaries.

Technical Architecture

How the System Works

The architecture combines computer vision, sequence intelligence, language processing, OCR, and speech synthesis. The goal is low latency, high interpretability, and broad device compatibility.

01

Visual Landmark Capture

MediaPipe Holistic extracts a dense set of hand, face, and body landmarks frame by frame to detect gesture geometry, motion direction, and expression-linked meaning.

02

Temporal Gesture Understanding

A sequence model such as Bi-LSTM or Transformer analyzes landmark progression over time, because sign meaning depends on motion, order, rhythm, and context rather than a single static pose.

03

Text and Language Mapping

The recognized gesture output is aligned to text tokens, while document content is processed through OCR and natural language understanding for translation, glossary generation, and simplified interpretation.

04

Speech and Learning Output

Speech synthesis modules can generate natural spoken output, while the learning layer delivers practice feedback, sign-linked explanations, and accessible content support.
